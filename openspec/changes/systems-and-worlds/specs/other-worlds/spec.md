## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Brown dwarfs
A fifth of drawn systems MUST be brown dwarfs, cooling with age, with fewer and lighter
planets than stars.

#### Scenario: an old brown dwarf
- **WHEN** a 0.05 M☉ brown dwarf is evolved to 1 Gyr
- **THEN** it is about 4×10⁻⁵ L☉ and 1550 K, an L or T dwarf about a Jupiter radius across

### Requirement: Companions have planets
A wide companion MUST be able to hold planets inside its own stable zone.

#### Scenario: a wide binary
- **WHEN** a binary is wide enough
- **THEN** its companion may have planets named after it

### Requirement: Thermal tides
A locked rocky world with 0.5–5 bar of air beyond the thermal-tide edge MUST turn
asynchronously.

#### Scenario: an Earth twin of a K dwarf
- **WHEN** an Earth twin with a bar of air orbits a 0.82 M☉ star at 0.62 AU
- **THEN** its solar day is about one Earth day

### Requirement: Ice shells
A world with an ice shell over an ocean MUST get tectonics, geology and elevation: young
ridged plains, chaos, bands and ridges on a thin shell, old dark and grooved terrain on a
thick one, with relief of hundreds of metres.

#### Scenario: Europa
- **WHEN** Europa is generated
- **THEN** about a third is renewed in many chaos patches, bands and ridges cross it, it
  keeps no basins and most of it lies within 500 m of the mean radius

### Requirement: Locked oceans carry heat
A locked world's ocean MUST carry the day side's heat across the terminator.

#### Scenario: a locked aquaplanet
- **WHEN** a locked ocean world turning in ten days is circulated
- **THEN** the sea east of the evening terminator is warmer than west of the morning one

### Requirement: No ice cliff
A world with weak heat transport MUST bring its ice edge in over latitudes.

#### Scenario: a fast spinner
- **WHEN** an Earth spinning in 8 hours is balanced
- **THEN** no two neighbouring degree bands differ by 4 K or more
