## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Compact inner systems
Small planets inside the frost line MUST form as chains whose neighbours' period ratios and
masses are like Kepler's multi-planet systems.

#### Scenario: Sun-like stars
- **WHEN** many Sun-like systems are formed
- **THEN** neighbouring small planets' period ratios have a median near 2 with few under
  1.2, about one small planet per star lies within 200 days, and neighbours' masses differ
  less than two planets from different systems

### Requirement: Scattering and migration
Pairs too close, or too eccentric by Petrovich's criterion, MUST scatter: merging when the
heavier planet's Safronov number is under 1, else ejecting the lighter and leaving the
survivor eccentric; some giant scatterings MUST end as hot Jupiters that clear the inner
system.

#### Scenario: radial-velocity giants
- **WHEN** many metal-rich Sun-like systems are formed
- **THEN** giants between 0.1 and 5 AU have a median eccentricity near 0.17, hot Jupiters
  are about 1% of stars and rarely have an inner neighbour

### Requirement: Tilts and moons
A rocky world's tilt MUST stay at its birth value only when a big moon steadies it.

#### Scenario: a moonless world
- **WHEN** a rocky world has no moon whose pull outweighs the star's
- **THEN** its tilt is drawn within 10–45° of its birth tilt

### Requirement: Trojans
A giant on a near-circular orbit MUST keep Trojans unless a heavier giant lies within 15
mutual Hill radii.

#### Scenario: the Solar System's giants
- **WHEN** Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus and Neptune are tested
- **THEN** Jupiter and Neptune have Trojans and Saturn and Uranus none
