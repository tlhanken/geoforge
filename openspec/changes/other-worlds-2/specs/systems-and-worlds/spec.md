## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Close pairs evolve together
A system's inner pair of stars MUST live through binary evolution when the heavier star, born
under 8 M☉, fills its Roche lobe before the system's age: stable transfer, a common envelope
or a merger by the donor's stage and the mass ratio.

#### Scenario: a giant swallows its companion
- **WHEN** a 1 M☉ star and a 0.4 M☉ star are born 0.7 AU apart and 12 Gyr pass
- **THEN** the pair is a helium white dwarf and a 0.4 M☉ red dwarf on a circular orbit under
  0.15 AU

#### Scenario: wide pairs
- **WHEN** a pair is too wide ever to touch
- **THEN** each star is what it would be alone, and the orbit has widened by the mass lost

### Requirement: A companion's light
A planet's mean flux MUST include the light of its system's other stars, at their separation
from its host, averaged over that orbit.

#### Scenario: Alpha Centauri
- **WHEN** a planet orbits one star of a 1.5 L☉ + B pair 23.4 AU apart with e 0.52
- **THEN** its flux is its own star's plus ~1/300 of Earth's from the other

### Requirement: Thick air holds a world off locking
A rocky world MUST turn with the solar day its air's thermal tide settles to when tides would
lock it but it has 0.5 bar or more of air beyond the thermal-tide zone's edge; the day is
longer for air past 5 bar, and such a world turns backward or forward by a draw of its own.

#### Scenario: Venus
- **WHEN** a world with 92 bar of air orbits a Sun-like star at 0.72 AU
- **THEN** its solar day is 100–135 days, and turning backward its sidereal day ~243 days
