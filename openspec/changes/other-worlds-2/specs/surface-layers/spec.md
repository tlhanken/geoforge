## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Methane hydrology
The methane cycle MUST fill the hydrology layer of a world with methane in its air, no
surface water and ground cold enough for liquid methane: rivers, lakes and watersheds from
rain and evaporation set by the cold trap, with no sea and lakes limited by the world's
liquid inventory.

#### Scenario: Titan
- **WHEN** the Titan archetype is generated
- **THEN** its lakes cover 0.2–3% of the surface, all poleward of 45°, and rivers run into
  them; the same seed gives the same lakes

### Requirement: Stacked gyres
A world with more than one eddy-driven jet per hemisphere MUST have a gyre front under each
band of westerlies, so its basins hold a stack of gyres; one jet keeps Earth's single front.

#### Scenario: a six-hour day
- **WHEN** a basin's winds are those of an Earth-sized world turning in six hours
- **THEN** each hemisphere has at least two fronts and the western boundary current runs
  poleward in at least two bands

### Requirement: Dunes
The dunes view MUST map sand seas on dry, flat land away from lakes and rivers on worlds with
rivers, as transverse, linear or star dunes by how the seasons' winds agree.

#### Scenario: the winds' agreement
- **WHEN** one wind blows all year, two equal winds blow at right angles, or the seasons'
  winds oppose
- **THEN** the dunes are transverse, linear or star dunes
