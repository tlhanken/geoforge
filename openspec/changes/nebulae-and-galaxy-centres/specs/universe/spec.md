## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Nebulae where their makers are
Nebulae MUST be found from the galaxy's stars and clouds — H II regions round young groups
with ionising stars, reflection nebulae round B stars in gas, dark clouds, planetary nebulae
and supernova remnants round the just dead — each identified by its maker, named, sized and
given its light, the same every time.

#### Scenario: round the Sun's place
- **WHEN** nebulae within 1.5 kpc of the Sun's place in a Milky Way are found
- **THEN** there are H II regions, reflection nebulae and dark clouds, 10–400 planetary
  nebulae and 1–80 supernova remnants within 1 kpc, each the size its kind allows

### Requirement: Galaxies have centres and globular clusters
Every galaxy MUST have a centre (a black hole by its bulge or stars, or none for some
dwarfs, and how active its nucleus is) and globular clusters by its halo's mass.

#### Scenario: the Milky Way's halo
- **WHEN** the globular clusters of a Milky Way halo are drawn
- **THEN** there are 120–260, three-fifths metal-poor, the metal-rich nearer the centre
