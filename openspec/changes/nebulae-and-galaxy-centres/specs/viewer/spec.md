## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Nebulae and galaxy centres shown
The viewer MUST draw the nebulae round a galaxy's home star and round a system, tell about
each and lead to its star, find them by name, and show a galaxy's centre and globular
clusters and which galaxies are active.

#### Scenario: round universe 7's home
- **WHEN** the nebulae of universe 7's home galaxy and system are asked for
- **THEN** several kinds are found, nearest first within the reach, each but the dark clouds
  leading to a star, searching one's name finds it, and the galaxy's centre and globular
  clusters are given
