## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: The just dead are stars of the field
The starfield MUST hold white dwarfs born within 25 kyr and neutron stars and black holes
born within 100 kyr as stars with addresses, gathered far off cheaply, without changing the
lit and dark stars drawn.

#### Scenario: a planetary nebula's star
- **WHEN** a planetary nebula is found within 1.5 kpc of the Sun's place in a Milky Way
- **THEN** its star resolves by its address and is a white dwarf dead under 25 kyr
