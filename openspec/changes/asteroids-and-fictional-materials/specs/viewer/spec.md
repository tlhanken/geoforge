## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Fictional materials shown where they are
The viewer MUST show a body's fictional deposits in its facts and as pins on its globe and
map, a system's finds, the systems round a star that hold any, and find them by search,
always flagged fictional; a faulty list MUST be reported, not fatal.

#### Scenario: a material on every world
- **WHEN** a universe lists a material found on any world with chance 1
- **THEN** the default body's facts and pins show it, its system lists it, most systems
  round it hold it, and searching its name finds the default system
