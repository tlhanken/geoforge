## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Asteroids have types and elements
Every belt member MUST have a spectral type drawn by its distance against its star's frost
line, an element makeup from its type's meteorite analog summing to one, a porosity and
density by size, and possibly a family sharing its type and orbit; the same member MUST
always be made the same.

#### Scenario: the Sun's main belt
- **WHEN** members 200–2200 of the Sun's main belt are made
- **THEN** S-types outnumber C-types inside 2.5 AU, C-types outnumber S-types beyond 2.8 AU,
  2–12% are M-types, and the Kuiper belt's members are icy

#### Scenario: an iron asteroid
- **WHEN** an M-type member is made
- **THEN** over 80% of its mass is iron and it holds platinum-group metals
