## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Every kind of star shown
The viewer MUST draw stars by kind (brown dwarfs, white dwarfs, neutron stars, black holes),
count them in the star view, and open any one's system.

#### Scenario: each kind
- **WHEN** a brown dwarf, a white dwarf, a neutron star and a black hole near the default
  star are opened
- **THEN** each system's scene comes back, with its star's notes
