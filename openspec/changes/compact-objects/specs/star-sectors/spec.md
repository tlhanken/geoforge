## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Brown dwarfs and dark remnants among the stars
The starfield MUST hold brown dwarfs (one to every four stars born) and the neutron stars
and black holes stars leave, in a bin of their own, without changing the lit stars drawn.

#### Scenario: near the Sun
- **WHEN** the stars within 40 pc of universe 7's default star are drawn
- **THEN** brown dwarfs are 15–25% of them, white dwarfs 1–6%, neutron stars 0.02–0.5% and
  black holes 0.01–0.3%
