## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: M dwarfs as bright as they are
A star's absolute visual magnitude MUST use a bolometric correction that holds for M dwarfs
down to 2000 K.

#### Scenario: nearby M dwarfs
- **WHEN** the correction is taken at Ross 128's, Proxima's, Wolf 359's and TRAPPIST-1's
  temperatures
- **THEN** each is within 0.3 mag of the value their luminosity and magnitude give
