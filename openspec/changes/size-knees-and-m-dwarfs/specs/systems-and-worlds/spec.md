## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Belt sizes steepen for the largest bodies
A belt's size law MUST allow a knee past which its slope steepens, and the Kuiper belt and
generated outer belts and Trojan swarms MUST use one.

#### Scenario: the Kuiper belt
- **WHEN** the Sun's Kuiper belt members are ranked by size
- **THEN** about ten are over 1000 km: the ninth is larger and the thirteenth smaller
